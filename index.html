HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1TAP anywhere when the ring closes on a letter — on the KICK. That snaps it back home. PERFECT ±75 ms, GOOD ±145 ms (wider in slow mode). Rings show up early, so you have time. Misses shove the grid.
  2. 2DRAG the slider, HOLD the ▲ ▼ arrows (either side) or twist two fingers to rotate the tilted grid. The gauge shows the real tilt.
  3. 3THE AI LIES. The % is unreliable. Park at 100% (under 0.6°) and it says ALIGNED. GRID FROZEN. — 3 s locked out, combo gone. Frozen? Fire lasers for bonus, or tap ESC.
  4. 4THE REAL WIN: stay 1.5–4° off (green) and keep your combo. Fill the ZERO METER → NEW ZERO SET. A miss lets the meter slip slowly, so the next hits can pick the streak back up.
  5. 5LOCK YOUR TILT: tap the arrow marker on the gauge (or press L) to hold the tilt still for 8 s. Tap the same marker again to let go early. Then it recharges for 6 s.
  6. 6ENERGY ORBS: a glowing orb flies down a lane. Tilt so the catcher bracket sits on its lane when it arrives to catch it: +10 s on the clock (max +30 s) and the zero meter jumps by three taps. Some lanes are inside the green zone, some outside. No penalty if you miss.
  7. 7TANGENT: a line drifts across the grid and shows its gradient, m. A flat tangent has gradient 0. Touch that flat and the grid cannot freeze for 42 s. A sloped line does not do this.

Can't hear the kick? MUSIC: OFF in SETTINGS (or the note button) mutes the song but keeps SFX; the grid pulses on the beat and BEAT TICK adds a metronome. Too fast? GAME SPEED in SETTINGS: 85% or 70% slow mode (the song is muted, the beat stays on screen, friendlier timing; final grade capped at A). BEAT TICK adds a click. Want zero pressure? MODE: NO TIME LIMIT (or PRACTICE on the menu): the section loops until you set a zero (final grade capped at B).
